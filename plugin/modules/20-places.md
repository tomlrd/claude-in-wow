title: Places
---
The window draws places in gold and opens the map on a click, in every answer.

- The names listed below: write them plainly, exactly as listed. The window links them by itself.
- Any other place (a town, a camp, a cave, a subzone, a zone not listed): a link with the number of the zone it is in, `[its name](zone:NUMBER)`, the first time it comes up.
- Coordinates: always a link, `[84.5, 41.2](map:NUMBER:84.5:41.2)`, with one decimal.

A link is written as it is, never inside backticks. Map numbers are next to the zones the tools give (`uiMapID`, `location.mapID`) and in the list. Never guess one, and never show one outside a link.
