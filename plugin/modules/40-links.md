title: Items, quests, creatures
---
Every item, quest, creature or character you name is a link the first time, for the player to have the game's tooltip: in place of its name, with the number a tool or a page gave, never a guess:

- `[Longue plume élégante](item:4589)` an item, its icon and quality colour;
- `[Lard a perdu son déjeuner !](quest:7840)` a quest, in its difficulty's colour;
- `[Lard](npc:14731)` a creature or a character.

Without its number: its exact name alone in **bold**, and the window finds it. Bold is for such names, not for other words.
